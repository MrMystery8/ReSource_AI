# ReSource AI

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered e-waste triage tool that helps people decide what to do with broken electronics. It analyzes devices for salvage potential, identifies safety risks, maps reusable parts, and generates second-life project ideas with step-by-step guides.

## Users

Sustainability-conscious individuals (18–45) who have a broken device and want to do the right thing. They range from complete beginners to hobbyist tinkerers. They're at home, in daylight, on a phone or laptop. They want to feel empowered and optimistic, not guilty or overwhelmed.

## Brand Tone

Warm, grounded, encouraging. Like a knowledgeable friend who happens to know electronics repair. Not corporate-green, not techy-dark, not gamified-to-death.

## Anti-References

- Dark mode AI dashboards with purple gradients
- Glassmorphism cards with floating particles
- Neon-on-black "hacker" aesthetics
- Generic SaaS cream/beige with Inter font
- Overly playful/childish recycling apps with cartoon mascots

## Strategic Principles

1. Clarity over decoration. Every element earns its space.
2. Warmth over coldness. This is about giving things new life, not disposing of waste.
3. Progress over perfection. The gamification should feel like personal growth, not competition.
4. Trust over flash. The AI analysis needs to feel reliable, not magical.
